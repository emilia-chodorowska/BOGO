How to order

Option 1: One-Time Loyalty Order (from the cart)

For members who processed and paid for a Loyalty Order (LRP) in the current or previous month. Your monthly LRP stays unchanged.

  1. 1Log in at shop.doterra.com and add the BOGO products with “Add to cart”.
  2. 2Hover over the cart icon and click “View your bag”. The PV tracking bar at the top of the page means the order qualifies as a One-Time Loyalty Order.
  3. 3Click “Continue to Checkout” and in the pop-up select “Continue with processing my One-Time Loyalty Order”.
  4. 4Check payment and delivery details, then click “Complete Order”.

The order can contain only BOGO products, no standard product needs to be added. Loyalty Points are earned from 50 PV.

Works only on shop.doterra.com, not on mydoterra.com.

It does not replace the monthly 100 PV LRP required to earn commissions (for Wellness Advocates). Source: doTERRA FAQ: One-Time Loyalty Orders.

Option 2: adding BOGO products to your Loyalty Order (LRP)

  1. 1Log in and click the account icon in the top bar.
  2. 2Open your Loyalty Orders (LRP) from the menu.
  3. 3Choose to manage the order to edit your LRP.
  4. 4Find the BOGO products with the search box or by browsing.
  5. 5Add the products and go on to the next step.

Remember: after adding BOGO items to your Loyalty Order, process the order right away to get the offer.

Remember: when choosing the full BOGO bundle, add at least one standard product to your LRP template so the order can be processed.
